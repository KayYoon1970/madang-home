DEBATE 04

Tangsuyuk: Pour or Dip?

Tangsuyuk is a popular Korean-Chinese dish made with crispy fried pork and served with a sweet and sour sauce.

Some people pour the sauce over the entire dish, making every bite juicy and flavorful. Others prefer to dip each piece into the sauce to keep the crispy texture.

Which side are you on?

WHY THESE DEBATES MATTER

Small choices. Big cultural clues.

These debates may look playful, but they reveal how Koreans think about relationships, boundaries, food preferences, seasons, and everyday social rules.

There is no single correct answer. That is exactly why the arguments never end.
